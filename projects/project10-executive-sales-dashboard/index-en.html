Project 10 – Executive Sales Strategy Dashboard (Excel)

Description

This dashboard was built to give executives and sales leaders an instant, data-driven view of business performance across regions, time periods and customer segments — all within a single Excel file.

What does this dashboard deliver?

  • A headline summary of the most critical KPIs: Total Sales (2,297K), Total Profit (286K), Profit % (12%), Average Order Value (459) and Total Orders (5,009) — giving leadership the big picture at a glance.
  • A monthly sales trend line chart covering all regions and years, making it easy to spot seasonal peaks (Q3–Q4) and slow periods.
  • A ship mode efficiency bar chart comparing Standard Class, Second Class, First Class and Same Day delivery volumes — helping operations teams optimise logistics costs.
  • A US regional map with colour-coded sales intensity, highlighting which states (California, Texas, New York) drive the most revenue.
  • A customer segment donut chart breaking down Consumer (50%), Corporate (31%) and Home Office (19%) contributions — guiding targeted marketing and sales strategies.
  • A Top 10 Most Profitable Products bar chart identifying the highest-margin items for prioritised promotion and inventory decisions.

In short: this dashboard turns raw transactional data into a strategic decision-making tool — enabling faster, more confident sales planning across regions, segments and product lines.

Recommendations (Data-Driven Action Plan)

  • Investigate the Sep–Oct revenue peak and replicate the conditions that drive it (campaigns, seasonal demand) in slower months.
  • Standard Class dominates shipping volume — evaluate whether a shift to Second Class for select orders can reduce costs without impacting customer satisfaction.
  • California and Texas are the strongest markets — deepen retention efforts there before expanding to lower-performing states.
  • Consumer segment accounts for 50% of revenue — create loyalty programmes and upsell paths for this group.
  • Canon and Fellowes products lead profitability — ensure they are always in stock and featured prominently in sales materials.
  • Home Office (19%) is the smallest segment but may have growth potential — test targeted B2C campaigns for remote-work products.

Technical Details (for Data Analysts / Recruiters)

  • Tool: Microsoft Excel
  • Data Structure: Transactional sales data with region, date, product, customer segment and shipping fields

Dashboard Components:

  • KPI Cards (5 metrics): Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit %, Avg. Order Value, Total Orders — calculated with dynamic formulas linked to slicer selections
  • Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart): aggregated by month across all years and regions, revealing seasonality patterns
  • Ship Mode Efficiency (Bar Chart): order count by delivery class — Standard, Second, First, Same Day
  • US Regional Map: heat-map style filled map chart showing state-level sales concentration
  • Customer Segment Distribution (Donut Chart): Consumer vs Corporate vs Home Office revenue share
  • Top 10 Profitable Products (Bar Chart): ranked by profit contribution
  • Interactive Slicers: Region (Central, East, South, West), Year (2014–2017), Quarter (Qtr1–Qtr4), Month (Jan–Dec) — all charts update dynamically
